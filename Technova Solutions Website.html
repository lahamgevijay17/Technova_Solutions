Case Studies

Real results from real clients

E-Commerce Demand Forecasting

RetailCorp Inc.

Challenge: Inventory misalignment causing 15% stockouts and excess inventory. Manual forecasting was time-consuming and inaccurate.

Solution: Implemented an LSTM-based demand forecasting model using 5 years of historical sales data, seasonal patterns, and external factors.

Technology: Python, TensorFlow, AWS SageMaker

✨ Result: 30% improvement in demand accuracy • 18% reduction in inventory costs • $2.3M annual savings

Customer Churn Prediction & Retention

TelecomNext Solutions

Challenge: High customer churn rate (8% annually) affecting revenue and market position. Needed proactive retention strategy.

Solution: Built a gradient boosting model identifying churn signals early, enabling targeted retention campaigns and personalized offers.

Technology: Python, XGBoost, Tableau, PostgreSQL

✨ Result: 25% reduction in churn • $5.1M revenue retention • 40% higher ROI on retention campaigns

Intelligent Document Processing

FinanceFlow Ltd.

Challenge: Processing 10,000+ documents monthly manually, error-prone and time-consuming. 6-week processing cycle.

Solution: Developed an AI pipeline combining OCR, NLP, and document classification to automate extraction and validation.

Technology: Python, PyTorch, Google Cloud Vision API, FastAPI

✨ Result: 90% automation rate • Processing time reduced to 2 days • 95% accuracy rate

Real-Time Sales Analytics Dashboard

GlobalTrade Partners

Challenge: Executives lacked real-time visibility into sales performance across 50+ locations. Reports took 3 days to generate.

Solution: Built an interactive BI dashboard with real-time data pipeline, predictive analytics, and custom KPIs.

Technology: Looker, BigQuery, Apache Airflow, Cloud Functions

✨ Result: Real-time insights • 85% faster decision-making • 22% increase in quarterly revenue
